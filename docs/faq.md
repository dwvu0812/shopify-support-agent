# Frequently Asked Questions — Northwind Outdoors

## How do I track my order?

Ask this chat "Where is my order #1001?" with your order number, or check
the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email.

## When will my order arrive?

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days ($6.95, free over $75).
Express shipping takes 1–2 business days ($14.95). International orders
take 7–14 business days.

## What is your return policy?

30 days from delivery, unused items in original packaging, free return label.
Refunds land 3–5 business days after the warehouse receives the return.

## Do you offer a warranty?

All products carry a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects. The
Summit 2-Person Tent and RidgeLine Waterproof Jacket carry a 2-year warranty.

## How can I contact support?

Email support@northwind-outdoors.example. Support hours are Monday to Friday,
9am–6pm PT. We reply within one business day.

## Do you have discount codes?

We run seasonal sales a few times a year. There is no permanent discount
code. Sign up for the newsletter on our website to hear about the next sale.

## Can I change or cancel my order?

You can change or cancel an order while it is still "processing". Once it
has shipped, use the 30-day return instead. Email support with your order
number as soon as possible.

## Is my payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Shopify Payments and Stripe. We never see or
store your card details.
