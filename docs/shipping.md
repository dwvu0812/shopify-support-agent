# Shipping Policy — Northwind Outdoors

## Where we ship from

All orders ship from our warehouse in Portland, Oregon, USA.

## Delivery times and cost

- Standard shipping: 3–5 business days, $6.95 per order.
- Express shipping: 1–2 business days, $14.95 per order.
- Free shipping: standard shipping is free on all orders over $75.
- International shipping: 7–14 business days, calculated at checkout by destination country.

Orders placed before 2pm PT Monday to Friday ship the same day. Orders placed
on weekends or public holidays ship on the next business day.

## Tracking

Every order gets a tracking number by email as soon as it leaves the warehouse.
You can check the status of your order any time by asking this chat for your
order number, for example "Where is my order #1002?".

## Delays

During peak season (November–December) standard delivery can take up to 7
business days. If your order has not arrived within 10 business days of the
estimated delivery date, contact support@northwind-outdoors.example.
